# Spontly — Product Brief

## One-liner
Real-time activity matching app that connects people who want to do the same thing right now but have no one to do it with.

## Problem
"I want to go hiking, but no one wants to come."
"I want to try salsa dancing, but I have no one to go with."
"I want to attend a workshop, but all my friends are busy."

This is not a personal problem — it's universal. A TikTok video where a girl said she feels completely alone received 100,000+ comments saying "same."

## Solution
Spontly lets users add activities they want to do. The AI finds other users nearby who want the same thing right now and connects them — instantly, safely, without swiping on looks.

## Key Differentiators
- **Activity-first, not people-first** — you choose what to do, not who to meet
- **Real-time intent matching** — not pre-planned events, but spontaneous "right now" moments
- **Safety built into the core** — GPS tracking, trusted contact alerts, AI mismatch detector
- **No Tinder mechanics** — no swiping on appearance, no awkward cold DMs

## Target Users
- International students in Korea (initial market)
- Young adults 18–35 in urban areas
- People who moved to a new city and haven't built a social circle yet
- Anyone who wants to try new activities but lacks company

## Market
- Social discovery apps: $5.5B globally, growing ~15% YoY
- Activities & experiences market: $1.2T globally
- Initial TAM: Seoul → Korea → Asia

## Business Model
| Stream | Details | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| Freemium subscription | Free: 3 matches/month. Premium: unlimited + filters | MVP |
| Venue partnerships | Cafés, gyms, studios pay for traffic | V2 |
| B2B team-building | Corporate access for company teams | V2 |

## Success Metrics (MVP)
- 1,000 waitlist signups before launch
- 500 MAU in first month (Hongdae/Itaewon area, Seoul)
- 60%+ of users complete at least one activity match
- 4.0+ average post-activity rating
